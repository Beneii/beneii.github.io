UTS Computing Science capstone · 2026

TokenMix: recurrent latent reasoning for arithmetic

A research and engineering project comparing answer-only, visible chain-of-thought and recurrent latent-compute GPT-2 variants on carry-balanced arithmetic.

The question

Can recurrent latent computation improve arithmetic reasoning without requiring a model to expose every reasoning step as text?

My contribution

I designed and evaluated the model variants, built deterministic data generation, training and evaluation workflows, and analysed accuracy, latency, carry-vector failures, adaptive routing and curriculum transfer.

Result

The five-digit curriculum experiment reached 99.25% accuracy. The project also documents architectural decisions, limitations and reproducible experimental evidence.

Tools

Python, PyTorch, GPT-2, transformer models, experiment design and model evaluation.
