Automation · Operations

Turning a three-hour process into ten minutes.

A practical automation project for a repetitive MYOB shop-sales workflow.

RoleDesign and implementation
ToolsAutoHotkey, MYOB
Outcome~3 hours to <10 minutes

01 · Problem

Manual repetition was consuming the workday.

The original workflow required repeated calculations, data entry and print actions inside an existing business application. It was slow, easy to interrupt and difficult to perform consistently.

02 · Approach

Automate around the real process.

I mapped the sequence, identified stable inputs and scripted the repetitive interactions. The solution was designed around the existing operational environment rather than requiring the business to replace its software.

03 · Result

A small system with a measurable impact.

The automated workflow reduced the process from roughly three hours to under ten minutes. More importantly, it made the task repeatable and freed attention for work that required human judgement.
