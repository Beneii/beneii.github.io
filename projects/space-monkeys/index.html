01 · Product

A platformer organised around elemental abilities.

Players move through a tutorial and four levels using Fire, Water, Earth and Air powers to solve movement challenges and collect resources.

02 · Delivery

Build systems that a team can integrate.

The project required gameplay code, level work, art and iteration to move together. We used shared task tracking and regular integration to keep the experience coherent across contributors.

03 · Outcome

Recognised beyond the classroom.

The subject coordinator selected the game for the 2025 UTS Student Games Showcase, where we presented it and collected feedback from attendees and industry guests.
