Embedded systems · Product craft

A physical product built from screen to shell.

Desk Companion combines software, electronics, animation and custom fabrication in one working object.

RoleEnd-to-end maker
ToolsESP32, C++, Fusion 360
DisciplinesSoftware, electronics, fabrication

01 · Intent

Make software feel like a character.

I wanted to explore how a small embedded device could feel expressive rather than purely functional. The project began as drawings and interaction ideas before any enclosure or electronics were committed.

02 · Architecture

Design every layer around the same object.

I modelled a negative volume for the electronics in Fusion 360, sculpted the exterior around it, programmed the ESP32 and created the on-device animations in Aseprite. Each layer constrained the next, so fit and interaction had to be considered together.

03 · Outcome

A complete, working prototype.

The final object houses the electronics cleanly, runs the custom animation system and demonstrates an end-to-end approach that spans software architecture and physical production.
